About us

We are a team from the College of Information Technology, University of Bahrain. We build analytics that small businesses can afford and understand.

Our mission

Give every small business in Bahrain affordable analytics, so decisions come from data rather than guesswork.

Our vision

A Bahrain where small businesses grow with confidence, using data tools that are simple and within reach.

What we aim to do

  • Understand what small businesses in Bahrain need from their data.
  • Build a simple mobile and web dashboard that meets those needs.
  • Measure usability, usefulness and forecast accuracy with real businesses.
